# Biomechanical Dream Archive atlas prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using existing fishtank
atlases only as layout, spacing, orientation, and pixel-rendering references.
The designs draw from the broader catalog of H. R. Giger without reproducing an
individual painting, sculpture, album cover, or branded prop exactly.

## Fish atlas

- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Asset: exactly 12 original fish in a strict 3-column by 4-row game atlas
- Style: high-resolution hand-pixeled biomechanical surrealism with crisp stepped
  edges, bone-metal anatomy, ribbed tubing, vertebral structures, pistons, cable
  tendons, and restrained tonal modeling
- Layout: one fully visible left-facing fish per cell with generous padding on a
  perfectly flat `#00ff00` production background
- Palette: bone ivory, gunmetal, charcoal, tarnished silver, surgical blue, and
  tiny rust-red warning accents
- Cells, left to right:
  1. Necronom Ray
  2. Li Spinefish
  3. Birth Machine Puffer
  4. Passage Angler
  5. Biomechanoid Surgeonfish
  6. Mordor Eel
  7. Spell Oraclefish
  8. Atomic Nursery Fish
  9. KooKoo Fanfin
  10. Brain Salad Crownfish
  11. Victory Lionfish
  12. Biomechanical Trainfish
- Constraints: no humans, infants, text, numbers, watermark, scenery, gore,
  sexual imagery, recognizable xenomorph heads, facehuggers, or copied artworks
